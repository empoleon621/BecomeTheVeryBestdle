Visual assets

Battle forest from Pokémon Showdown (https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/fx/bg-forest.png).
Showdown credits Kyle Dove for battle backdrops (https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/credits).
Full Restore, Revive, and Max Revive item sprites from Pokémon Showdown
(https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/itemicons/).

Ninja Adventure by Pixel-Boy and AAA, CC0 (https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack).
CC0 dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The overworld rooms use tiles by Akizakura16, Shiney570, and UltimoSpriter
from the public Gen 5 interior tileset. Tiles were cropped, resized, and
arranged into new room layouts for BecomeTheVeryBestdle.
https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/15/#:~:text=Gen%205%20interior%20tileset

2D Pokémon sprites by contributors to the Smogon Sprite Project (https://github.com/smogon/sprites).
Paldea battle animations come from Lucidious89's Animated Pokémon System graphics pack, exported
to WebP from the published sprite strips. Paldea artwork and animation credits include the Smogon
Scarlet/Violet Sprite Project and the Sprites Animados contributors listed by the pack authors:
https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/1544/
https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/1831/
Additional 2D animations by Ghasty001, used with credit:
https://github.com/Ghasty001/Animated_sprites_by_Ghasty001

The player and trainer overworld frames come from user-provided sheets:
Pokémon Diamond/Pearl Lucas sheet (sheet credit: Kaori);
Pokémon Diamond/Pearl Gym Leaders & The Elite Four (sheet credit: Naokohiro);
Pokémon HeartGold/SoulSilver Trainers (Overworld) (sheet credit: Draboon);
Pokémon Black 2/White 2 Overworld Entities.

The named Gen 4 and Gen 5 sprite collections by PurpleZaffre were used to
identify the frames. PurpleZaffre also drew the Lorelei, Agatha, and Wallace
sprites. Sprite references: https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/609/
and https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/619/.
Drake, Glacia, Phoebe, and Sidney frames come from Emerald Essentials
(https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/1689/). Credits to Xysmaniac for conversion
and assembly, pret for pokeemerald, huderlem for Porymap, and grunt-lucas
for Porytiles.
Some movesets were adapted from the Smogon Strategy Dex
(https://www.smogon.com/dex/sv/pokemon/).

HeartGold/SoulSilver UI artwork from user-provided sheets:
Text Boxes (sheet credit: Lemon);
Fonts (sheet credit: Jackster);
HP Bars (sheet credit: GameMaster12 and StayPuft).
